Industries/Power Systems & Grid
Power Systems & Grid

Grid-code compliance, from PSCAD to IEEE 2800 in one pipeline

Run PSCAD/EMTDC electromagnetic transients, PSS/E transmission power flow, and OpenDSS distribution studies in one open toolchain, with IEEE 2800 and NERC PRC-029 ride-through verification built in.

Get started Open Playground
msx — power-systems
$ msx pscad verify-ieee2800 SolarPlant_250MW.pscx --tstop 3.0 --step 20us -o report.json
$ msx psse powerflow case.raw
$ msx psse to-pscad case.raw
The challenge

What slows power systems & grid teams down

01

Interconnection queues

Inverter-based resources need extensive EMT and ride-through studies before they can connect.

02

Black-box vendor models

OEM controller DLLs only run inside specific desktop tools on specific operating systems.

03

Fragmented studies

Transmission, distribution, and EMT models are maintained separately and drift apart.

Capabilities

What ModelScript does for you

EMT simulation

Native PSCAD project ingestion and electromagnetic transient solving.

Ride-through verification

Automated IEEE 2800 LVRT/HVRT and NERC PRC-029 checks with JSON reports.

Power flow & DER hosting

PSS/E transmission cases and OpenDSS unbalanced distribution and hosting-capacity analysis.

COMTRADE waveforms

Read and write IEEE C37.111 transient recordings for comparison against field data.

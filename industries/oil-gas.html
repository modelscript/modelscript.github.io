Oil, Gas & Subsurface

Flow assurance from reservoir to export pipeline

Connect ECLIPSE reservoir decks, OLGA multiphase flow models, and PipelineStudio networks, and simulate transients, phase envelopes, and gas properties in one workspace.

Get started Open Playground
msx — oil-gas
$ msx olga simulate subsea_tieback.opi --duration=3600 --out=traj.csv
$ msx olga flash --comp="C1:0.75,C2:0.10,C3:0.08,nC4:0.04,C7:0.03" --p=50 --t=295.15 --envelope
$ msx olga diagram tieback.opi --view=profile --out=profile.svg
The challenge

What slows oil, gas & subsurface teams down

01

Siloed disciplines

Reservoir, wells, pipelines, and facilities teams each use different simulators.

02

Costly transients

Slugging, shutdown, and restart studies need many long runs across scenarios.

03

Hard-to-audit decks

Large keyword decks are difficult to review, lint, and version-control.

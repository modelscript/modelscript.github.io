1. Davis SuiteSparse Left-Looking Cholesky (LLT) ​

Academic Citations ​

  • Davis, T. A. (2006). Direct Methods for Sparse Linear Systems.
    SIAM. DOI: 10.1137/1.9780898718881
  • Chen, Y., Davis, T. A., Hager, W. W., & Rajamanickam, S. (2008). "Algorithm 887: CHOLMOD, supernodal sparse Cholesky factorization and update/downdate."
    ACM Transactions on Mathematical Software, 35(3), pp. 22:1–22:14. DOI: 10.1145/1462166.1462176

ModelScript Rationale & Modifications ​

Solves symmetric positive-definite sparse systems Ax=b arising in finite element analysis, electrical mesh equations, and quadratic programming subproblems.

  • Modifications: Left-looking column-by-column factorization computing non-zero patterns dynamically via elimination trees; operates inside unmanaged WebAssembly linear memory with zero heap fragmentation.

2. Gilbert-Peierls Left-Looking Sparse LU (PAQ=LU) ​

Academic Citations ​

  • Gilbert, J. R., & Peierls, T. (1988). "An approach to sparse LU factorization based on graph coloring and topological sort."
    International Conference on Supercomputing.
  • Davis, T. A. (2006). Direct Methods for Sparse Linear Systems. SIAM. Chapter 5.

ModelScript Rationale & Modifications ​

Factorizes unsymmetric, highly irregular sparse Jacobians generated by torn physical DAE systems.

  • Modifications: Depth-first search (DFS) topological ordering to find the exact non-zero structure of each column in L and U in time proportional to arithmetic operations; threshold partial pivoting with diagonal preference to preserve physical sparsity.

4. Buchberger's Gröbner Basis Engine with Gebauer-Möller Criteria ​

Academic Citations ​

  • Buchberger, B. (1965). An Algorithm for Finding the Basis Elements of the Residue Class Ring of a Zero Dimensional Polynomial Ideal.
    PhD thesis, University of Innsbruck.
  • Gebauer, R., & Möller, H. M. (1988). "On an installation of Buchberger's algorithm."
    Journal of Symbolic Computation, 6(2-3), pp. 275–286. DOI: 10.1016/S0747-7171(88)80048-8
  • Cox, D., Little, J., & O'Shea, D. (2015). Ideals, Varieties, and Algorithms (4th ed.).
    Springer. DOI: 10.1007/978-3-319-16721-3

ModelScript Rationale & Modifications ​

Solves small non-linear algebraic loops analytically to avoid numerical Newton iterations entirely. Computes the Gröbner basis under lexicographic monomial ordering (Lex) to triangularize polynomial systems into univariate root problems.

  • Modifications: Implements the Gebauer-Möller criteria (Bnew and Mnew sets) to eliminate over 90% of redundant S-polynomial reduction checks; operates over exact rational coefficients Q.

1. Moving Horizon Estimation (MHE) over the Linear-Memory DAE Arena (arena-mhe.ts) ​

MovingHorizonEstimator reconciles streaming physical sensor windows (TelemetryWindow over [t−W,t]) against the in-memory DAEBuilder arena by solving a box-constrained trajectory-tracking problem:

minθ,w12∑k=0N−1‖ysim(tk;θ,w)−ymeas(tk)‖22+12∑iλi(θi−θiprior)2+λw2∥w−wprior∥22s.t.θmin≤θ≤θmax,F(x˙,x,y,θ,w,t)=0
  • Joint Physical & Neural Hybrid Calibration: Simultaneously estimates physical DAE parameters θ and embedded ArenaNeuralBlock surrogate weights w inside the same optimization vector.
  • Adjoint-Driven L-BFGS-B: Evaluates exact objective gradients via DaeAdjointSolver (solveDaeAdjoint) and converges in ≤25 quasi-Newton lbfgsbSolve iterations, automatically warm-starting initial states from window boundaries t0.

2. Unscented Kalman Filter (UKF) & Multi-Strategy Parameter Calibration (calibrator.ts, bayesian_calibrator.ts) ​

ModelScript provides two complementary calibration engines for offline test-stand identification and online probabilistic tracking:

  • Joint-State Unscented Kalman Filter (BayesianParameterCalibrator): Augments the continuous state vector x∈RNx with unknown physical parameters θ∈RNp into z=[xT,θT]T∈RNx+Np. Propagates 2L+1 deterministic sigma points (α=10−3,β=2,κ=0) through the nonlinear DAE transition zk+1=faug(zk,uk,Δt) and measurement model yk=haug(zk) (UnscentedKalmanFilter), extracting full posterior covariance Pk|k, 95% confidence intervals (θ^i±1.96σi), and automated Digital Thread hypergraph write-back suggestions.
  • Deterministic & Global Metaheuristic Calibration (ModelicaCalibrator in calibrator.ts): Supports Levenberg-Marquardt (lm) with damped normal equations (JTJ+λI)Δθ=−JTr, Sequential Quadratic Programming (sqp) with BFGS Hessian updates, scalable adjoint lbfgsb, Bayesian MCMC / global evolutionary search (cmaes, de, pso), and two-stage hybrid-cmaes-lm global-to-local polishing.

3. Page-Hinkley / CUSUM Statistical Residual Drift Detection (drift-detector.ts) ​

CusumDriftDetector continuously monitors multi-channel innovation residuals rc(t)=ymeas,c(t)−ytwin,c(t) to detect incipient actuator degradation, sensor bias, or structural drift while rejecting zero-mean Gaussian measurement noise:

  • Two-Sided Sequential Cumulative Sum: Normalizes each channel residual z~c=(rc−μc)/σc with minimum detectable shift slack kc=δc2σc (default δc=1.5σc) and updates positive and negative CUSUM statistics:Sc+(t)=max(0,Sc+(t−1)+z~c(t)−kc),Sc−(t)=max(0,Sc−(t−1)−z~c(t)−kc)
  • Adaptive Burn-In & Severity Classification: Supports empirical baseline calibration (calibrateFromData) and triggers structured DriftEvent alerts when max(Sc+,Sc−)≥hc, classifying severity (low, medium, high, critical) by threshold ratio Sc/hc alongside online shift magnitude estimation.

4. Virtual Sensor Synthesis & Remaining Useful Life (RUL) Prognostics (prognostics.ts) ​

  • Virtual Sensor Synthesis (VirtualSensorSynthesizer): Evaluates unobservable internal physical states (e.g., rotor hotspot temperatures, internal valve spool stresses) at arbitrary timestamps t directly from the continuous DenseHermiteCheckpointTape.
  • Physics-of-Failure Damage Accumulation (RulPredictor): Computes instantaneous degradation rates dDdt under three selectable damage laws:
    1. Arrhenius Thermal Aging: dDdt=Aexp(−EakBT)
    2. Miner's Power-Law / Basquin Fatigue: dDdt=kfatσm
    3. Archard Linear Mechanical Wear: dDdt=kwearσ
  • Probabilistic RUL Quantiles: Given current damage D(t)∈[0,Dcrit] and operational duty-cycle load variability σload, projects log-normal survival quantiles:RULP50=Dcrit−D(t)D˙(σduty),RULP10=RULP50exp⁡(−1.28155σload),RULP90=RULP50exp⁡(+1.28155σload)

Upstream & Downstream Pipeline Connections ​

  • Upstream Inputs:
    • Linear matrix systems from TornBlock and TR-BDF2.
    • 3D CFD and FEA continuum field snapshots from CFD and FEA.
    • Live telemetry streams (CSV, ASAM MDF4, MCAP) and DAEBuilder adjoint sensitivities.
  • Downstream Consumers:
    • Embedded into real-time Modelica plant loops via msx surrogate and operational digital twins.
    • Feeds calibrated posterior parameters and RUL health metrics back into the DigitalThreadHypergraph.
    • Accelerates linear solves across all numerical integrators and optimal control routines.
